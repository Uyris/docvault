# Acme Robotics — Employee Handbook (Excerpt)

## 1. About the company

Acme Robotics was founded in **2014** and is headquartered in **Porto Alegre,
Brazil**. The company designs autonomous warehouse robots and the software that
coordinates them. As of this handbook revision, Acme employs **420 people**
across engineering, operations, and customer success.

## 2. Working hours and remote work

Acme operates on a hybrid model. Employees may work remotely **up to three days
per week**; the remaining two days are spent at the office. Regardless of
location, everyone is expected to be reachable during **core hours, which run
from 10:00 to 16:00 (BRT)**. Time outside core hours is flexible.

## 3. Paid time off

Full-time employees accrue **30 days of paid vacation per year**, accruing at a
rate of 2.5 days per month. Unused vacation may be carried over, but the balance
may not exceed **45 days** at any time. Vacation requests should be submitted at
least **two weeks in advance** through the people portal.

## 4. Equipment and home-office stipend

Every employee receives a **company-issued laptop**. Remote workers are also
entitled to a one-time **home-office stipend of R$2,000** to set up their
workspace. The stipend is requested through the operations team and is paid with
the following month's salary.

## 5. Information security

Multi-factor authentication (**MFA**) is **mandatory** on all company accounts.
Passwords must be rotated every **90 days** and must be at least **12 characters**
long. Company data may never be stored on personal devices. Suspected security
incidents must be reported to the security team within **one hour** of discovery.

## 6. Customer support SLAs

Support tickets are triaged by severity. The response-time targets are:

- **Critical** (production down): first response within **2 hours**, 24/7.
- **High** (major feature broken): first response within **8 business hours**.
- **Normal** (minor issue or question): first response within **2 business days**.

Resolution targets are tracked separately and reviewed monthly by the customer
success team.

## 7. Learning and development

Each employee has an annual **learning budget of R$3,000** for courses,
conferences, and books. Unused budget does not carry over to the next year.
Conference travel must be approved by the employee's manager in advance.
